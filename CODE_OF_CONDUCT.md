# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all people, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

We pledge that participation in this community will be a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Be Respectful**: Treat all contributors with respect and kindness
- **Be Inclusive**: Welcome people of all backgrounds and skill levels
- **Be Constructive**: Provide helpful and constructive feedback
- **Be Professional**: Maintain professional and courteous communication
- **Be Patient**: Help others learn; remember that everyone is learning
- **Give Credit**: Acknowledge the contributions and ideas of others
- **Be Open-Minded**: Consider different perspectives and approaches
- **Report Issues**: Bring up concerns through appropriate channels
- **Focus on Code**: Discuss ideas and solutions, not personalities

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment**: Any form of harassment, discrimination, or bullying
- **Abuse**: Verbal abuse, insults, or derogatory comments
- **Discrimination**: Any form of discrimination based on protected characteristics
- **Sexual Harassment**: Unwanted sexual attention or advances
- **Threats**: Threats of violence or harm
- **Spam**: Posting spam or irrelevant content
- **Trolling**: Deliberately posting inflammatory or off-topic messages
- **Doxxing**: Sharing private information without consent
- **Exclusion**: Deliberate exclusion based on any characteristic
- **Disruptive Behavior**: Deliberate disruption of community discussions or activities

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to:

- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct
- Ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful
- Explain the reasoning behind moderation decisions when appropriate

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the repository maintainers:

**Contact Methods:**
- GitHub Issues: Create a private security advisory (if available)
- Email: Contact the repository owner directly through their GitHub profile
- Comment: Tag repository maintainers in issues or PRs

**When Reporting, Please Include:**
- A clear description of what happened
- Specific examples or quotes when possible
- Who was involved
- When the incident occurred
- Any additional context

**Confidentiality:**
All reports will be reviewed and investigated confidentially. We will not publicly identify the reporter unless they request it.

## Consequences

Depending on the severity of the violation, actions may include:

1. **Verbal Warning**: A private message explaining the issue
2. **Temporary Suspension**: Temporary restriction from participation
3. **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from the community

Repeated violations or severe misconduct may result in immediate permanent removal.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All community spaces within this repository (issues, PRs, discussions, wiki)
- Community interactions related to this project
- Official community events or gatherings
- Any space where members identify as representing this community

This Code of Conduct does not apply to spaces outside the community or interactions unrelated to this project.

## Commitment to Learning

We are committed to:

- Educating ourselves and others about inclusive practices
- Being open to feedback and willing to improve
- Creating a learning environment for all skill levels
- Supporting contributors in understanding and following this Code of Conduct
